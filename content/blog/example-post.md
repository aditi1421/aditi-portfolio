Everything above the second `---` is frontmatter. `title`, `date`, and `summary` show
up on the blog index. `draft: true` keeps a post hidden from the site; remove that line
to publish.

The filename becomes the URL, so `example-post.md` lives at `/blog/example-post`.

## Headings work

So do **bold**, *italic*, [links](https://example.com), and `inline code`.

- Bullet lists
- Numbered lists
- Tables, blockquotes, and code blocks
