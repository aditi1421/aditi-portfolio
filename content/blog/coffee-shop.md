I love coffee, and got used to drinking americano, no sugar.

I am writing this while sitting at one of my favorite coffee shops, araku in Indiranagar. Coffee is truly an experience and something that has evolved over years instead of just coming in a single day.

My assumption is that this coffee shop is preferred by many people because of the occupied seats, and overhear many people describing coffee and the intricate taste of it.

Coffee is an experiment, and some coffee shops excel at it while some are okayish. India has had a wave of people admiring coffee and I don’t think this came in a single day, rather it evolved over time. With experiments, with actual people giving feedback of what would people buy v/s dislike.

I think the same is valid if you compare it to other areas like AI progress, like how do we make AI intelligent. Can we throw a lot of compute and expect it to magically be intelligent tomorrow and solve all world problems? solve hunger? solve poverty?

If we call AI so intelligent, why has it not given a solution to solve the world’s major problems that humans still suffer from? I would expect someone who does it to be intelligent because of the ever changing factors, humans, economy, taste.

Coffee is an acquired taste. Varieties of coffee were introduced in India because people went away (exposure) and brought back that experience to share with other people, which evolved over time locally with exquisite indian flavours and localised names. What works in Italy may not work in Australia.

I can’t just throw coffee beans and expect a different taste, or coffee taste to suddenly change one day without the consumer and the feedback loop of what is good vs bad. Can i extrapolate coffee progress in India? From filter coffee to Black coffee which was adopted by people over time and with more money, because the economy grew.

Coffee improves through repeated interaction, feedback, adaptation and exposure to real world preferences.

I was inspired to write this while reading whether intelligence explosion will be in a vacuum
