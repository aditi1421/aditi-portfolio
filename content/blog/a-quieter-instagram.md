title: "A Quieter Instagram"
date: "2026-08-01"
summary: "Instagram does too many things in one app. What would it look like if the photos you share with friends lived somewhere quieter?"
---

Why do we have so many things in a single app?

I open Instagram to look at a friend's photos from a trip. Twenty minutes later I close it, having watched four reels, a cooking video and something an AI made, and regret it later. It's a loop.

I don't like the way instagram is. It's a very popular app but I have issues of how bad it is, how it wastes your time and how it has too many things going on. I don't like it. I deactivate it, but then I come back to it to know updates of friends I haven't been in touch with or because some friend is in my town. It has reels, and it has photos you can post about your trip, birthday, friends. On the same app, you can make ai slop reels, growth reels or cooking reels. Why are both of the things happening in the same app?

Many people create a finsta/spam account, which is basically an alternate id to their main account, where they have their close friends and mainly post photos and reels about their personal life.

I recently saw this tweet on X, where someone had built a photography website and they could put all their pictures on it, and share it with friends. I found it pretty cool. Why don't we have this?

I can just share this website/app with my friends, and they can see all the pictures I want to share without having to "like", "comment". Do we really need to count how many likes someone got on a picture?

The obvious question here could be a) People want to see how many people viewed your pictures, otherwise it may feel like talking to an empty room. But, you could just display who viewed your pictures, like an instagram story does.
