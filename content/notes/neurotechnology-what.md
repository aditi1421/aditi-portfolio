title: "Neurotechnology what?"
date: "2026-10-08"
---

- Ok so the fastest path towards achieving something even remotely is having “non-invasive BCI” at scale. Scale is important because with less data, signal mixes with noise and you don’t really get good information out. Invasive BCI is the north star, but because of regulations, privacy and ethics it will take a longer duration to achieve that on scale compared to what we can do with non-invasive BCI.
- There have been papers on the ability to reconstruct photos from brain signals, which means that similar signals correspond to specific parts of the brain. Looking at photos/color/video activates a certain portion of neurons
- The aim with BCI is to raise human capability rather than containing artificial intelligence by banning the AI models.
- Reading and writing is great and that would be a nice to do, but currently brain gives a lot of signals that we can read and infer stuff. Writing will come later on
- We still have not stress tested non-invasive BCI’s, they are mainly in the academia world with a bit of industry
- Things to think about is how can we convince consumers to put a bci machine on their heads and go about their day, we already give our heart data and steps through watches, but it will be a huge privacy issue as well if we can translate cognition → thoughts (pretty cool though)
- The brain automatically is able to assess stress and dial it down, or suppress notifications when it knows the person is in flow state
- Maybe scaling laws can hold for brain data, and brain mapping can be done starting with broad levels
- BCI can synchronize with the environment and get context, act as per the human, but this can go the opposite way, so it is important that the neuroethics and policy are aligned to humans
- The interface needed to interact with AI is old, we need new interface that is socially acceptable and has on device pre-processing. It might create this problem of humans having the crisis of “identity of self”
- Overall, i do believe that non invasive BCI is a good starting point → Invasive BCI. With scale, AI has become advanced on extracting information and should be able to cut noise and understand the human’s intent. Generalization of different brains might be an open problem? Each brain is different.
